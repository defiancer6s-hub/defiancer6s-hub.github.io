ClipFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Summary

ClipFlow is a personal desktop app that runs on the user's own computer. It has no servers of its own, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Everything it stores stays on that computer.

Information ClipFlow accesses

When you connect a platform account, ClipFlow receives, through the platform's official API and only with your permission:

  • TikTok: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and your current posting options (nickname, username, available privacy settings, whether comments/duets/stitches are allowed, maximum video length), plus the status of posts ClipFlow submits for you. Scopes requested: user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload.
  • YouTube: your channel's identity and the status of videos ClipFlow uploads for you.
  • Access tokens issued by these platforms so ClipFlow can act on your behalf.

ClipFlow also works with the video files and text you choose inside the app.

How it is used

Only to do what you ask: show which account is connected, upload the videos you select with the captions and settings you review, check whether they were published, and keep a local history of your posts. ClipFlow does not sell, share, rent or use this information for advertising or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

  • Post drafts, schedules and posting history are stored in a local database on your computer.
  • Access tokens and app credentials are stored in your operating system's secure credential store (macOS Keychain / Windows Credential Manager), never in plain text.
  • Video files stay on your computer and are sent only to the platform you choose, when you choose to post.

Sharing

Data leaves your computer only when sent to the platform you are posting to (TikTok or YouTube), or to an AI provider you optionally enable for caption suggestions, in which case only the clip description and settings you provide are sent. No data is sent to the ClipFlow developer.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect an account at any time in ClipFlow's settings, which revokes the access token with the platform and deletes it from your computer. You can also revoke access from your TikTok or Google account settings. Deleting ClipFlow's data folder removes all locally stored information. Content already posted to a platform is managed in that platform.

Children

ClipFlow is not directed at children and is intended for use by its developer.

Changes
