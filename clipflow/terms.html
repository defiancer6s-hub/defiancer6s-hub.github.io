1. What ClipFlow is

ClipFlow is a personal, non-commercial desktop application that helps its user prepare short gaming videos and publish them to their own accounts on platforms such as TikTok and YouTube, using those platforms' official APIs. It runs locally on the user's own computer. It is not a hosted service and is not offered for sale.
